Questions

What do you offer?

For authors: editing, proofreading, Kindle cover design, KDP formatting, blurbs, bios and publisher outreach. For professionals and small businesses: CVs, cover letters, elevator pitches, presentation scripts and business proposals.

How do I pay?

Card or PayPal, bank transfer (EFT) or crypto. You'll see the options at checkout.

Do you write legal agreements?

No. I do not draft contracts or give legal advice. For anything legally binding, please use an attorney.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. You approve a fixed price before any work begins.

How do I get a book I bought direct?

After payment is confirmed we email your file. If you'd rather read on a Kindle, use the Amazon button.

Is my work private?

Your manuscript and documents are used only for your project and are never shared or sold.
