Questions

What kind of problems can you help with?

Paperwork, tech trouble, writing, book and cover design, and money-saving advice. If it's not on the list, ask. If we can't do it we'll say so and point you elsewhere.

How do I pay?

Card or PayPal, bank transfer (EFT) or crypto. You'll see the options at checkout.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. You approve a fixed price before any work begins.

How do I get a book I bought direct?

After payment is confirmed we email your file. If you'd rather read on a Kindle, use the Amazon button.

Is my information private?

We only use what you share to solve your problem, and we never sell it.
